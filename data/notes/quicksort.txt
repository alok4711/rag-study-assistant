Quicksort

Quicksort is a divide-and-conquer sorting algorithm. It picks a "pivot"
element from the array and partitions the other elements into two
sub-arrays: those less than the pivot and those greater than the pivot.
The sub-arrays are then sorted recursively.

Time complexity: average case O(n log n), worst case O(n^2) when the
pivot choice is poor (e.g. already sorted array with first-element pivot).

Space complexity: O(log n) due to the recursion stack, since quicksort
sorts in place.

Quicksort is generally faster in practice than merge sort due to better
cache performance and lower constant factors, despite having a worse
worst-case time complexity.
